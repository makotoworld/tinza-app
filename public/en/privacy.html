Privacy Policy

Last Updated: February 19, 2025

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how we handle user personal information in the mobile game application "Tinza" (hereinafter referred to as "the App").

2. Information We Collect

The App may collect the following information:

  • Advertising Identifier (IDFA): Used for ad delivery
  • Gameplay data (scores, play time, etc.)
  • In-app purchase information
  • Device information (OS version, device model, etc.)

3. Purpose of Information Use

The collected information is used for the following specific purposes:

  • Providing game features and service improvement
    • Improving game experience through gameplay data analysis
    • Bug fixes and performance improvements
  • Delivering personalized advertisements
    • Displaying ads based on user interests
    • Measuring and analyzing ad effectiveness
  • Processing in-game purchases
    • Providing premium features
    • Managing purchase history
  • Resolving technical issues and customer support
    • Analyzing error reports
    • Providing user support

4. About Advertisements

The App uses Google AdMob to deliver advertisements. The advertising identifier (IDFA) may be used for the following purposes:

  • Providing more relevant ads based on user interests
  • Measuring and analyzing ad performance
  • Optimizing ad display for better user experience
  • Monetization for continuous app development and improvement

Users can limit ad tracking through device settings. Basic app features remain available even with tracking disabled.

5. Data Protection

Collected data is protected with appropriate technical and organizational measures. However, we cannot guarantee 100% security for data transmission over the internet.

6. Data Sharing

Collected information will not be shared with third parties except in the following cases:

  • When required by law
  • With user consent
  • When necessary for ad delivery
  • When necessary for processing in-app purchases

7. Children's Privacy

The App does not intentionally collect personal information from children under 13 years of age.

8. Changes to Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as needed. Users will be notified of significant changes through in-app notifications or other appropriate methods.

9. Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy, please contact us through the App Store.
